Mathematical & Physical Foundations

$$\text{Kepler's Third Law: } T^2 = \frac{4\pi^2}{G M_*} a^3 \implies T \propto a^{3/2}$$
$$\text{Orbital Speed: } v_{orb} \approx \sqrt{\frac{G M_*}{a}}, \quad \text{Escape Velocity: } v_{esc} = \sqrt{\frac{2 G M_p}{R_p}}$$
$$\text{Maxwell-Boltzmann Mean Thermal Velocity: } v_{th} = \sqrt{\frac{8 k_B T_{eq}}{\pi m}}$$
Atmospheric Retention Rule of Thumb: A planet retains a gas over geological timescales ($10^9\text{ yrs}$) if $v_{esc} > 6 \times v_{th}$. If $v_{esc} \le 6 v_{th}$, thermal escape (Jeans escape) rapidly strips the atmosphere into space.
